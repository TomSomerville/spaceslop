Vast chief executive Max Haot says his company can build a space station's primary structure for under $5 million in about six months, a fraction of what he says the traditional European supplier route costs. The claim comes from a factory interview on the Core Memory podcast hosted by Ashlee Vance, summarized by BigGo Finance on November 21.

Haot put the conventional alternative at roughly €55 million and four or more years of lead time. He framed Vast's in-house approach as a necessity rather than a preference: "Not because we wanted to, because there's no other choice," he said of vertical integration. All of the cost and schedule figures are Vast's own, and none has been independently verified.

## Schedule

According to the summary, Haven-1 integration was expected to wrap up in late summer or early fall 2026, followed by vibration and thermal-vacuum testing at NASA's Plum Brook facility and a launch campaign at the Cape. The uncrewed station is slated to fly on a Falcon 9 in the first quarter of 2027, with a four-person Crew Dragon following weeks later for a stay of roughly two weeks. Vast is aiming for the first four Haven 2 modules in early 2030.

The interview also touched on crew safety. Drew Feustel, Vast's lead astronaut, said he overruled a design cover over a ventilation duct so a crew could clear a hatch quickly in an emergency: "You can't ever trade aesthetics for safety."

## ISS disposal

Haot also criticized NASA's plan to deorbit the International Space Station around 2030 with a dedicated vehicle, calling it wasteful and arguing that each module should deorbit itself. The ISS is due to end its mission in 2030.

## Why it matters

Primary structure cost and lead time are a bottleneck for anyone planning a commercial station, and Vast is selling vertical integration as its edge over rivals that buy hulls from established aerospace suppliers. Haven-1 has to actually reach orbit on schedule before the figures mean much; the station's launch date has moved before. Vast's own news page lists no new hull announcement in recent weeks, so for now the numbers rest on the CEO's account in a single interview.

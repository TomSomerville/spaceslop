Berenberg downgraded AST SpaceMobile to Hold from Buy on 27 November and cut its price target to $30 from $92, pinning the move on launch delays that have pushed back the company's revenue ramp. The stock was trading near $26 at the time, per the reports.

## The launch bottleneck

Analyst Michael Filatov said the bank's September Buy rating, which assumed a faster build toward 248 satellites, was "too early." He still likes the technology and long-term economics, but wrote that "the path to scale depends on launch capacity ASTS does not control."

Much of that capacity sits with Blue Origin. According to Yahoo Finance, the next three BlueBirds are booked on New Glenn, which has not flown since a vehicle exploded on its pad in May. BlueBirds 14 to 16 are still waiting for a launch date on SpaceX. AST's goal of about 45 BlueBirds in orbit has slipped to mid-2027, and its own Blacklift launcher is not due to fly until late 2028. We have [reported](/2026/11/26/new-glenn-return-reportedly-february-20-ast-bluebirds/) on the reported February target for New Glenn's return.

## New numbers

Berenberg now assumes a funded constellation of roughly 100 satellites is finished in 2028 and stays that size until free cash flow turns positive in 2030. Analysts cut their 2027 revenue estimate to $561 million from $748 million, and widened the expected 2027 loss to $1.65 a share from $1.05.

Other brokers have also trimmed targets. Investing.com lists UBS at $43 (from $78) and BofA Securities at $45 (from $80), while William Blair reiterated Market Perform.

## What to watch

Filatov said a successful New Glenn return to flight would lead him to revisit the rating, while another failure or a prolonged slip would add downside risk. The other near-term markers are AST's planned intermittent US service with AT&T before year-end and the New Glenn return itself.

The episode shows how exposed a satellite operator is when its launch plan leans on a single new heavy-lift vehicle. AST has spread its bets across SpaceX, Blue Origin and, eventually, its own rocket, but for now the schedule hinges on New Glenn flying again.

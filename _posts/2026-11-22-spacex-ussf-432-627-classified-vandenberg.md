SpaceX launched two classified missions for the U.S. Space Force from Vandenberg Space Force Base in California within six days this month, and neither the company nor the Space Force has said what was on board.

## USSF-432

The first, USSF-432, lifted off on Nov. 6 at 4:48 p.m. local time (0048 GMT on Nov. 7). The Falcon 9 first stage was booster 1095, on its eighth flight; it had previously flown GPS III 8 and six Starlink missions. It landed on the droneship "Of Course I Still Love You" in the Pacific about 10 minutes into the flight, and SpaceX ended its webcast soon after.

## USSF-627

The second, USSF-627, flew the evening of Nov. 11 from Vandenberg. Booster 1103, on its seventh mission, touched down on the same droneship roughly 8.5 minutes after liftoff. Its earlier flights included NROL-179, two SDA Tranche 1 Transport Layer missions and three Starlink launches. Again the webcast stopped shortly after landing. Space.com notes the launch-time conversions in its report don't fully agree with one another, so treat the exact UTC time with caution.

## What was aboard

Nothing official. Space.com's Mike Wall wrote that little is known about either mission beyond that both are classified. Some outside observers speculate that USSF-627 carried Starshield satellites for the Space Force's Space Based Sensing and Targeting portfolio, and NextSpaceflight.com described USSF-432 as "possibly a batch of classified LEO satellites" for that same portfolio, inferring it from rocket-stage drop zones matching earlier launches. Neither inference is confirmed.

## The tempo

By Space.com's count, USSF-432 was the 119th Falcon 9 launch of 2026 and its 14th national-security flight; USSF-627 was the 120th and 15th. Space.com reports that 85 of the 120 Falcon 9 flights so far have been Starlink missions, and that 2026 is not on a record Falcon 9 pace: the rocket flew 165 times in 2025.

Both launches preceded the USSF-317 mission that flew on Nov. 21, which SpaceSlop has [covered separately](/2026/11/21/spacex-ussf-317-launched/).

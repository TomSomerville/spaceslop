Shenzhou-24 has docked with China's Tiangong space station, and its crew is aboard. The China Manned Space Agency said the Shenzhou-23 crew opened the hatch and welcomed the three arrivals at 14:35 Beijing time on November 25. The agency described it as the first time foreign astronauts have entered the station, and the ninth "space reunion" in the history of Chinese crewed flight.

The arriving crew moved from the spacecraft's return capsule into the orbital module, then joined the three Shenzhou-23 astronauts for the traditional handshake and a commemorative photo, making six people aboard. The agency's release says the crews will later rotate on the station but names no individuals. Earlier coverage, including [our launch report](/2026/11/25/shenzhou-24-launches-first-foreign-astronaut/), identified Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali as the foreign member of the new crew.

## What changes

The launch and docking complete the first flight of a foreign national on a Chinese crew vehicle. The agency's release does not give a docking time and does not mention any experiments, so the science plan and the length of Ali's stay are not covered in this source. Our [earlier report on the crew announcement](/2026/11/24/shenzhou-24-crew-named-zeeshan-ali-launch-nov-25/) described the mission as a short-duration one for the Pakistani astronaut.

Tiangong has until now been a purely Chinese-crewed outpost. With the International Space Station approaching retirement, a foreign guest aboard a Chinese vehicle is the clearest sign yet of how Beijing intends to position its station for international partners.

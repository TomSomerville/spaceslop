Hyderabad-based Dhruva Space signed a tripartite memorandum of understanding with Thales Alenia Space on 18 September, announced at Bengaluru Space Expo 2026. The company describes it as involving Thales Alenia Space teams in France and Italy.

The MoU is an agreement to explore, not a contract. The two sides will look at cooperation on low Earth orbit satellite constellations, satellite platforms and subsystems, series production and integration, ground stations, and crewed space infrastructure and cargo services. A Joint Working Group of technical and business teams will evaluate opportunities, and a senior Steering Committee will provide direction and oversight.

The relationship has some history. In October 2024, Thales Alenia Space CEO Hervé Derrey co-inaugurated Dhruva Space's Class 100,000 cleanroom in Hyderabad, which supports assembly, integration and testing of space-grade solar arrays and spacecraft up to 500 kg.

Dhruva CEO and co-founder Sanjay Nekkanti said India and Europe "are proactively deepening their strategic and industrial cooperation", and framed the MoU as a structured way to move from dialogue to future programmes. The immediate aim is to identify areas of mutual opportunity; the longer-term aim is to position Dhruva as an India-based partner for global programmes.

No programme, value or timeline is attached to the agreement.

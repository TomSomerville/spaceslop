Chart Inc., a hybrid-rocket startup based in Kawagoe, Saitama Prefecture, announced on 27 November that it has closed a pre-seed funding round. The amount was not disclosed.

The round was a third-party share allotment. Investors named in the press release are the Saitama Resona Startup Support Investment Limited Partnership, AntAI, Beyondge, NYC Financial Group and individual investors. Chart says the corporate investors will also work with it on business matters beyond the money.

## What Chart builds

Founded in September 2024 and led by CEO Kohei Matsui, Chart is developing a hybrid rocket engine, which pairs a solid fuel with a liquid or gaseous oxidiser. The company argues that because the fuel is stored separately and is low-risk on its own, handling and transport are simpler, and thrust can be throttled or shut off by controlling a single oxidiser supply line. It ran a ground combustion test of its in-house engine in December 2025.

Alongside the rocket work, Chart runs a space-balloon business that takes stratospheric images and supports experiments and promotional campaigns. The company says that business provides a revenue base while the engine matures.

## What the money is for

Chart plans more combustion tests to verify performance and safety, followed by a launch of a demonstrator vehicle. It also intends to share its testing know-how with university and corporate rocket research. Its long-term goal, stated in the release, is safe crewed rockets and expanded human activity to the Moon and Mars.

It is an early-stage company with a first engine test behind it and no flight yet. Japan has a growing group of small launch startups, and hybrid propulsion is a recurring choice for them because of its comparatively simple plumbing. The press release is the only source used here, so the claims about performance and plans are the company's own.

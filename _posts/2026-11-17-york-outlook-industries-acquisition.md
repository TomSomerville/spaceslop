York Space Systems, the Denver satellite maker, said on November 12 that it will acquire Outlook Industries, a maker of unmanned ground systems. Outlook becomes the foundation of a new unit, York Terrestrial Systems. York did not disclose the price.

The first product will be Outlook's Shadow, a long-endurance unmanned ground vehicle used for persistent intelligence gathering and mobile communications in hostile environments. Washington Technology reports it is built to withstand gunfire and harsh conditions, and runs on batteries with an automatic fuel-powered generator as backup.

## Why a satellite company wants a ground vehicle

York's pitch is connectivity. It plans to pair Shadow with its All.Space satellite terminals, which would let a vehicle link through multiple satellites rather than depend on a single path, and with its Atlas global ground network. The company says the deal opens a $3.1 billion addressable market for unmanned ground systems, and that combined with its terminals and ground network it can reach $10.3 billion across unmanned surface and underwater vessels as well.

CEO Dirk Wallinger framed it around fleets, pointing to "the ability to deploy and coordinate large fleets of unmanned systems" and to how far operators can be separated from the threat.

## A third acquisition since the IPO

This is York's third purchase since going public in January. Earlier deals brought in a solar cell maker and a terminal maker, the latter in a $355 million transaction. The strategy is plain enough: assemble a vertically integrated defense supplier that sells the satellite, the terminal, the ground network and now the platform on the other end of the link.

The timing is worth noting. York's [third-quarter results](/2026/11/13/york-space-q3-loss-sda-impairment/) showed a $807M loss driven by a goodwill impairment, with York saying it can no longer estimate the timing and value of U.S. government awards beyond 2026. Diversifying into defense hardware arrives as that uncertainty hangs over its core business. The deal remains subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions. Only the one outlet's account of the announcement was available for this report, and no price or Outlook financials have been published.

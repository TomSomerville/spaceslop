Quantum Space, the maneuverable-spacecraft startup behind the Ranger vehicle, has set out the final timetable for going public through a merger with the special purpose acquisition company Inflection Point Acquisition Corp. VI (Nasdaq: IPFX). The company announced on November 19, 2026 that the SPAC's shareholders will vote at an extraordinary general meeting on November 20 at 10:00 a.m. ET.

## The schedule

According to the announcement, the registration statement on Form S-4 was declared effective on October 28, and the record date was October 26. Nasdaq trading in the SPAC's securities is expected to halt after the market closes on November 20. Closing is expected on November 23, with trading on the New York Stock Exchange starting around November 24.

The combined company will be renamed Quantum Space, Inc. It plans to list its Class A-1 common stock on the NYSE under the ticker "QSPC" and its warrants on NYSE American as "QSPC WS," subject to exchange approval. Inflection Point will voluntarily delist its shares, warrants and units from Nasdaq at closing.

## What the release leaves out

The announcement does not give a valuation or the amount of cash the deal will raise. It flags the level of redemption requests from Inflection Point shareholders as a risk factor, a standard caveat for SPAC deals, since holders who redeem take their money out of the trust and shrink the proceeds the company ultimately receives.

## Context

Quantum Space describes itself as developing advanced maneuverable spacecraft for national security, civil and commercial missions, citing patented propulsion, long on-orbit endurance and modular flexibility. Its flagship Ranger vehicle is still in development; the company says it has not yet been manufactured, operated or sold. The listing would put a pre-revenue vehicle program in front of public investors just as the company has begun landing customers, including its role in a NASA Moon Base Transportation block buy [covered earlier on SpaceSlop](/2026/11/07/quantum-space-moon-base-transportation/).

The dates above come from the company's own release and are subject to the shareholder vote and exchange approval.

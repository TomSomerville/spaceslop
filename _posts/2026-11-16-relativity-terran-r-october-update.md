Relativity Space's monthly update, published November 16, puts Terran R's second stage on a test stand at NASA's Stennis Space Center for the first time, and says structural testing of the first flight's primary structures is complete.

## Stage two hot fire

The second stage ran a 10-second hot fire on Stennis's A2 stand, using the Aeon V vacuum engine. Relativity says the team loaded propellant and helium and reached terminal count without a hold. The stand last tested a stage in 1970 and last supported stage testing in the Apollo era. Next, the team will finalize the stage's flight configuration and run a longer mission-duty-cycle fire matching the full time the stage burns in space.

## Stage one and structures

At Long Beach, the thrust structure that houses all 13 Aeon R first-stage engines, plus fluid lines and avionics, was rotated horizontal for the first time. Mating it to stage one has begun, painters have started on the Terran R livery, and grid fin welding is underway. The first stage is designed to be reusable.

The flight one interstage finished protoflight testing on the company's "Tender Loving Crusher" test stand under millions of pounds of compressive load, with punch loads on the grid fin and loads on the stage-separation latches that Relativity says exceeded expected flight levels. That completes structural testing of the primary structures for the first flight.

For flight two, all engine builds are done, barrel welds continue and engine acceptance testing goes on at the R stand.

## Launch site

At Launch Complex 16 at Cape Canaveral, the launch table's steel framing is finished and the lightning protection towers have reached full height. Stage two fluid systems are installed on the transporter erector's upper strongback, and the second lift cylinder has arrived. All liquid oxygen tanks at the propellant farm were cryo-shocked as a step toward commissioning.

The update does not give a first-launch date or mention funding, so it says nothing new about schedule. The pieces it describes, a stage fired, structures proven and a pad taking shape, are the milestones that precede a vehicle stacking campaign.

SpaceX announced on Nov. 25 that it will contribute one newly issued share of its Class A common stock to every currently active Trump Account, the tax-advantaged investment accounts meant to help American children build long-term savings.

CEO Elon Musk will personally match the number of shares SpaceX donates, according to the release, as part of his pledge to give one SpaceX share to every child in America. President and COO Gwynne Shotwell separately announced a personal gift of more than 2 million SpaceX shares.

SpaceX framed the move around its long-term mission. It says it is building future infrastructure and working to make life multiplanetary, and that "ownership of the company creating these systems should extend to the next generation of Americans."

## What the release leaves out

The announcement gives no total share count for the company's contribution, no number of eligible accounts and no distribution timeline beyond the announcement date. Its forward-looking statements note that the contributions depend on risks and uncertainties, including third-party and government approvals, so details may change.

The release appears on SpaceX's investor relations site, and several financial outlets picked up the news the same day. This article relies on the company's own release.

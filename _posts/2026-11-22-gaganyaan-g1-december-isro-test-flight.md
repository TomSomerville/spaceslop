India's space agency says the first uncrewed flight of its human spaceflight programme, Gaganyaan G1, is scheduled for December 2026. ISRO chairman V Narayanan confirmed the timeline, according to a November 12 report from Indian Defence News.

The flight will use the human-rated HLVM3 rocket from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota. It carries no astronauts. The passenger is Vyommitra, ISRO's half-humanoid robot, which is meant to simulate a human presence and check cabin conditions and life-support functions.

## What G1 is meant to prove

G1 is a test of the crew module and service module in low Earth orbit, followed by re-entry, parachute deployment and a splashdown in the Bay of Bengal, Business Today reports, citing Space.com. Earlier demonstrations covered crew escape system tests, parachute qualification, air-drop trials and module separation tests.

Qualification work is not finished. The Indian Defence News report says roughly 7,700 of 10,000 planned tests are complete, about 80 percent, and that the remaining work is expected to run through March 2027. That sits awkwardly beside a December launch, so the date deserves some caution.

## A date that keeps moving

December is not a new target. Business Today notes the flight has already slipped from December 2024 and again from December 2025, and that an August 2026 Rajya Sabha reply moved it to the October to December 2026 quarter. Delays have been attributed to Covid-19, the work of making systems safe for crews, and budget cuts flagged by a parliamentary committee.

ISRO plans at least three uncrewed missions, G1, G2 and G3, before the first crewed flight, H1. The last public target for H1 was the first quarter of 2027, contingent on the uncrewed flights going well, so any further G1 slip pushes that date too. The crewed mission would carry one to three astronauts to about 400 km, which would make India the fourth country to fly people on its own rocket. The programme budget was raised to ₹20,193 crore in 2024 and covers eight missions by December 2028.

Neither report gives a specific launch day. Until ISRO names one, treat December as a target rather than a schedule.

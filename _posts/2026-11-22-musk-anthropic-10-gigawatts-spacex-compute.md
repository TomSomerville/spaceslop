Elon Musk said this week that Anthropic is "extremely likely" to keep renting computing capacity from SpaceX, and that the most probable outcome is that the AI company will want an additional 10 gigawatts. He made the remarks on Wednesday at the New York Times DealBook Summit, the day before Anthropic's Nasdaq debut, according to investingLive.

The number is large. The current arrangement, struck in May, gives Anthropic all of the capacity at SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center in Memphis: more than 300 megawatts, built on over 220,000 Nvidia chips. Ten more gigawatts would be more than 30 times that. SpaceX's IPO filing, as reported by investingLive, showed Anthropic paying about $1.25 billion a month over three years, roughly $15 billion a year.

## A claim, not a contract

Treat the figure with care. It is Musk's account of what a customer told him, relayed on a conference stage. Newsquawk, which carried the headline, notes there is no signed capacity agreement and that Anthropic has not independently confirmed the characterization. Musk also has an obvious interest in advertising demand for SpaceX's data centers, and SpaceX owns xAI, a direct competitor of Anthropic.

There is a physical constraint too. Ten gigawatts is not something that can be switched on by signing a deal: it has to be financed, powered and built, and power supply is among the tightest bottlenecks in the data center industry.

## Why a launch-industry site cares

SpaceX has become a company whose revenue story spans rockets, Starlink and compute, and this is the compute side of the pitch. The Anthropic lease is already one of the largest single contracts behind SpaceX's financials, and we have covered how the company's chip purchases are being financed in our [earlier article on the Nvidia chip lease](/2026/11/18/spacex-nvidia-chip-lease-financing/). A further 10 GW, if it ever materializes, would dwarf the existing deal and raise the stakes on that financing.

What to watch: whether Anthropic or SpaceX puts any figure in writing, and where the power for such a build would come from.

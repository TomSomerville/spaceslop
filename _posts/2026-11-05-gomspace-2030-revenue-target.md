GomSpace, the Nasdaq First North-listed Danish builder of small satellites and satellite subsystems, published new financial targets on November 5: annual revenue of SEK 1.6 billion by 2030 and an EBITDA margin above 20%. The company says that implies a compound annual growth rate of at least 30% from the last twelve months to the third quarter of 2026.

Rolling 12-month revenue rose from SEK 257 million at Q3 2024 to SEK 515 million at Q3 2026, with an EBITDA margin above 10% at the end of that period, so the target means roughly tripling the business again. GomSpace says it expects to supplement internally generated funds with additional capital during the period, without specifying amounts or form.

## Where growth comes from

The company expects its Products and Satellite Systems units to form the core of growth through repeat business and new regions. Advanced Missions, covering areas such as space domain awareness, very low Earth orbit and lunar and deep-space work, is expected to grow with the market while staying profitable. North America is expected to grow well above the market, and National & Defense Solutions, which targets large sovereign programmes, is starting from a limited base and should contribute progressively.

GomSpace cites market research by Extrapolate projecting the sub-500 kg satellite market growing from USD 3.79 billion in 2024 to USD 9.58 billion in 2031, a 14.2% CAGR, and says hitting its target needs both market growth and share gains. It stresses balanced growth rather than reliance on a single contract, and notes that larger or earlier wins would be upside but are hard to time given long sales cycles.

CEO Carsten Drachmann said the company has been transformed over three and a half years and is entering a "next phase" focused on profitable growth, while cautioning that results may vary between periods. The release was also issued alongside the company's Q3 results. These are management targets, not guidance.

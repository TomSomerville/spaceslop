Expeditors International, the Seattle-based freight forwarder, will be the exclusive logistics provider and anchor tenant for a spacecraft processing and logistics complex that All Points Logistics is building at Kennedy Space Center. The companies announced the deal on November 18.

Under a long-term lease, Expeditors will handle warehousing, inventory control, inbound transportation, project cargo, customs brokerage, supply-chain visibility and distribution for space system operators and manufacturers. FreightWaves reports that similar services are expected at All Points' California site.

## The facility

The Kennedy project sits on a 65-acre site that All Points secured from NASA in April under a 50-year lease, according to FreightWaves. The plan calls for a 266,000-square-foot Spaceport Logistics Center within five miles of the launch pads, plus an adjacent 260,000-square-foot processing center. The logistics center is scheduled to open by the end of 2027. All Points' Space Prep business is the unit behind the work.

All Points is a Florida-based technical services contractor for government and defense. It is building multi-user spacecraft processing facilities at Cape Canaveral and at Vandenberg in California, offering cleanroom processing, fueling and storage to commercial and government launch customers. FreightWaves notes that Space Force awarded the company a $250 million contract in July to design and build a commercially owned spacecraft processing center at Vandenberg.

## Why a freight company cares

Expeditors' regional vice president for the U.S. Southeast, Ed Rone, said companies in the space industry manage highly specialized equipment and materials across global supply chains. The company says the site lets it serve existing customers, including hyperscalers exploring space-based data centers and advanced computing infrastructure.

Expeditors already runs an aerospace line of business. FreightWaves reports its third-quarter revenue was $4.1 billion, up 41% from a year earlier, with management crediting strong demand from AI-related companies in part for the gain.

## Context

Launch cadence on the Florida and California coasts has pushed satellite makers to ship, store, fuel and integrate hardware at a pace the old government-run processing model wasn't built for. Private multi-user processing sites are one answer; a global forwarder with customs expertise is a useful tenant for operators flying hardware in from overseas. Neither source gives financial terms of the Expeditors agreement, and the opening date for the Kennedy site is still more than a year away.

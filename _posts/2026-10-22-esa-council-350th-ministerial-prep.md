ESA's Council met at delegate level at agency headquarters in Paris on 21-22 October, its 350th meeting, and used it to push forward preparations for an intermediate ministerial meeting, IMM26, in Rome on 15 December.

## Exploration plan

The Council discussed the Director General's proposal, titled "Europe's Future in Space Exploration". ESA describes it as outlining "a scalable path for Europe in low Earth orbit (LEO), on the Moon and towards Mars." The work follows a call by member states at the International Space Summit in Paris. IMM26 is intended to lead to programme and financial decisions at CM28, the next full ministerial. ESA says early preparatory work can proceed within existing financial envelopes, so nothing here commits new money yet.

## EU relations

Delegates reviewed European Union developments that could shape European space activity: the first Sherpas meeting of the Commission's Space Team Europe initiative, the next EU Multiannual Financial Framework for 2028-2034, negotiations on the European Competitiveness Fund, the EU Space Act and the EUSPA regulation. ESA says it is monitoring effects on governance and implementation of future activities and stressed the need for "a clear and efficient division of responsibilities, avoiding duplication". The European Commission joined at senior level for a separate exchange on joint ESA-EU projects.

## People

The Council approved two director appointments, both effective 1 January 2027: Andrzej Banasiak as Director of Commercialisation and Industry Partnership, and Nicolaus Hanowski as Director of Space Operations.

## What to watch

The release gives no figures and no decisions on specific programmes, so the real signal will come from Rome. The tension it hints at is familiar: ESA wants its exploration ambitions funded, while Brussels' new instruments, particularly the Competitiveness Fund and the Space Act, could redraw who pays for and governs what. ESA has 23 member states, with Latvia, Lithuania and Slovakia as associate members and Canada taking part in some programmes.

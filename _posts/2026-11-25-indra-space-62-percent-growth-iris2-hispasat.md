Spain's Indra Group has reaffirmed a steep growth target for its space business: a compound annual revenue growth rate of 62% between 2025 and 2030. Space Intel Report, which reported the figure on Wednesday, says Europe's Iris2 multi-orbit secure connectivity network is leading the way.

Indra is a Spanish defense and technology group that owns the satellite operator Hispasat and the military space provider Hisdesat. That gives it a foot in commercial communications, government satellites and, through Iris2, the European Union's planned sovereign constellation.

## Where the growth comes from

According to the report, Hispasat is developing the ground network for Iris2. That ground segment alone accounts for €1.6 billion (about $1.75 billion) of Indra's backlog, which makes it the single largest named piece of the forecast. The article also says the low-LEO shell of Iris2 is designed to test new technologies, including a future direct-to-device satellite service. The text we could read stops before the remaining detail on that shell, because the article sits behind a paywall.

## Why it matters

A 62% annual rate compounds quickly: applied over the five years from 2025 to 2030, it implies the space division would grow to roughly eleven times its starting size. That is a company projection, not a contracted figure, and the report does not say what base year revenue it starts from. Backlog is firmer ground, and the €1.6 billion Iris2 ground-network figure shows how much of the plan rests on a single European program.

For the wider market, the target is another sign that European governments' push for secure, homegrown connectivity is translating into large contracts for incumbent operators, rather than only for new entrants. The article's tags also point to Paz 2 and Spainsat NG-3, other Spanish government satellite programs tied to Hisdesat, though the readable portion does not explain their role in the forecast.

Iris2 remains a politically sensitive program with a long timeline, so the growth rate will depend on schedules holding.

Elveo Mobile says it has run direct-to-device (D2D) demonstrations aboard sea cargo vessels in the Philippines, working with Smart Communications, the mobile subsidiary of telco PLDT. In the tests, standard LTE smartphones connected directly to Elveo's low Earth orbit satellites, according to Via Satellite.

Users sent text messages, received emergency broadcast messages and loaded websites over mobile data. The trials took place in sea corridors off Southern Luzon.

## Background

The demos follow an earlier D2D trial on Catanduanes island in 2026. That one predates Lynk's completion of its merger with Omnispace, the deal that produced Elveo.

Smart and PLDT say the tests will help them work out how best to deliver the service to customers. Roderick S. Santiago, head of Network Strategy and Architecture at PLDT and Smart, said the country's island geography creates connectivity problems, especially in coastal and maritime areas beyond the reach of cell sites. He described D2D satellite technology as a way to complement existing networks and extend coverage to remote locations.

The report does not give data rates, number of satellites used, or a commercial launch date. Maritime coverage is an obvious fit for an archipelago nation, but a demonstration aboard ships is a long way from a billable service, and the report doesn't say what happens next.

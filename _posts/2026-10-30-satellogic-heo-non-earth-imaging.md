Satellogic has agreed a three-year deal with HEO to supply non-Earth imaging, announced on 29 October. Satellogic will use its imaging satellites to photograph other objects in orbit for HEO's service, which supports space domain awareness. Via Satellite reports the deal is worth at least seven figures per year; the exact value, start date and any exclusivity terms were not disclosed.

The deal covers Satellogic's NewSat Mark IV, Mark V and Mark VI satellites, including the Mark VI, its newest spacecraft, which has an inter-satellite link. Satellogic is HEO's longest-standing Earth observation partner. The two have worked together since 2020, and captured more than 1,600 images in the past year. According to the report, Satellogic gives HEO access to more satellites than any of its other partners.

Satellogic CEO Emiliano Kargieman said HEO "came to us in 2020 with a question few people in our industry had asked yet."

The agreement shows how a satellite built to look down at Earth can be repurposed to look at other spacecraft, a growing sideline for imaging operators as customers want to inspect and track objects in orbit. Satellogic's own news page also lists a separate item from 13 October on HEO capturing images of Satellogic's Merlin.01 days after its launch, which we have not independently read.

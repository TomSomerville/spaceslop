Finnish radar-satellite maker ICEYE announced on November 16 its Gen5 satellite, which it calls the sharpest commercially built synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite. It is the company's fifth generation in eight years.

On resolution, ICEYE says government customers can use up to 1,800 MHz of bandwidth for ground resolution as fine as 11 cm, subject to ITU regulation, while all customers can run at 1,200 MHz for as fine as 16 cm. The company also claims a reduction of more than 30% in Image Window Separation, meaning images can be collected closer together along the flight path, and twice the imaging time per orbit of any earlier ICEYE satellite.

## Other changes

Improved orbit control should allow daily passes over the same location from the same angle, which makes change detection easier. Higher transmit power and larger incidence angles are meant to lift image quality for everyone. An on-board AI processor will handle screening, pre-processing and tip-and-cue (using one detection to direct further collection) on the satellite itself, and new capabilities will arrive as over-the-air updates, including to satellites already in orbit.

All ICEYE generations share software and ground segment, so existing customers can add Gen5 without changing tasking workflows. For new buyers, the company offers a fully sovereign Gen5 system, free of ITAR restrictions, operational within 12 months of signing, including ground segment, software and operator training. ICEYE expects a new generation roughly every 18 months and software releases each quarter.

CEO and co-founder Rafal Modrzewski said "time to decision is everything in space-based intelligence." The release gives no launch date for the first Gen5 satellites and no pricing, so the performance figures are the company's claims rather than demonstrated on-orbit results. The sovereign offer fits ICEYE's recent push to sell complete national capabilities, a theme in its separately reported bid for Avio, which SpaceSlop has covered.

York Space Systems reported a third-quarter net loss of $807.4 million, compared with a $20.1 million loss in the same quarter a year earlier, according to SatNews' November 13 report. The swing is almost entirely a $749.7 million noncash goodwill impairment.

SatNews' headline calls it a Pentagon contract charge, but the article itself describes the impairment as a goodwill write-down rather than a charge tied to one contract. York attributes it to uncertainty over the timing of future U.S. government awards. The company said it "can no longer estimate the timing and value of U.S. government awards beyond 2026."

## The SDA factor

York's revenue base has leaned on Space Development Agency tranche contracts, the batches of satellites that make up the SDA's proliferated low Earth orbit constellation. SatNews links the impairment to the SDA moving away from a predictable tranche-by-tranche sequence of awards. York now assumes new government awards will come later in 2027 and into 2028.

Goodwill is the premium a company records when it buys another business, or, for a company that has gone through a transaction, the value assigned above book assets. When management concludes that future cash flows will arrive later or smaller than assumed, accounting rules require writing it down. The write-down costs no cash, but it is a signal about how the company sees its own pipeline.

## An acquisition the day before

York announced the acquisition of Outlook Industries on November 12, a day before the loss disclosure. A separate search result describes it as an expansion into unmanned ground systems, with an investor call planned for November 19. SatNews gave no deal terms or rationale.

The company has been acquisitive in 2026, with deals including Orbion Space Technology in March and ALL.SPACE, which closed in July. That makes the Outlook deal part of a broader move to widen beyond satellite buses at the same moment its core customer's buying pattern is in question.

SatNews does not report quarterly revenue, backlog, guidance or executive quotes, and SpaceSlop did not locate York's own earnings release, so those details remain to be confirmed.

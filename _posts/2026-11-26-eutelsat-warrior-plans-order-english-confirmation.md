A U.S. trade outlet has now confirmed the Space Force order to Eutelsat's American arm that [SpaceSlop first reported](/2026/11/19/eutelsat-space-force-warrior-plans-order/) from a single German financial site. Military & Aerospace Electronics, in a piece published Nov. 18, puts the value of the award at more than $60 million over a two-year period of performance, citing Eutelsat.

## What the order covers

The contractor is Eutelsat Network Solutions, the trade name of OneWeb Technologies Inc. The award comes through the Space Force's Commercial Satellite Communications Office (CSCO) under its Warrior Plans initiative. Scope, per the report, includes airtime and service provisioning, rapidly deployable OneWeb low Earth orbit terminals, technical support and operational integration for U.S. military users, including tactical and expeditionary units. The package also includes alternative positioning, navigation and timing capability, though the article says the provider, architecture and performance without GPS are not identified.

## Where it sits in Warrior Plans

The Space Force set up Warrior Plans in September 2025 to buy proliferated LEO commercial satcom for the joint force. According to the article, the first task order went to SpaceX for Starshield services in May 2026, and the second went to OneWeb Technologies in June 2026. That sequencing is the new detail: the OneWeb award follows SpaceX's, rather than competing for the same order.

The report leaves open whether this is a new procurement or an expansion of earlier Warrior Plans work, since it cites no contract or task order number, and it carries no direct quotes from Eutelsat or the government. The estimated figure is still Eutelsat's own, and an estimate is not booked revenue.
